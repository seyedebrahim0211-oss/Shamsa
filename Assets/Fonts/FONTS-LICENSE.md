# Bundled fonts

`Vazirmatn-*.ttf` — Vazirmatn by Saber Rastikerdar and contributors.
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
Source: https://github.com/rastikerdar/vazirmatn

The fonts are embedded unchanged as WPF resources so Shamsa's Persian UI looks the same on
every machine, whether or not the font is installed. Under the OFL they may be bundled and
redistributed with software; they may not be sold on their own.
